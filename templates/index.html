Getting Started

Welcome to HealthHero! Start by chatting with our AI health coach, tracking your daily metrics, setting reminders, and viewing your health analytics. Use the quick action buttons for fast logging.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my health metrics?

▼

Go to the Tracking section and select a metric type, enter the value, and add optional notes. You can also use quick action buttons in the Chat view for faster logging.

How do reminders work?

▼

Create reminders in the Reminders section. Set a title, type, and scheduled time. Reminders help you stay on track with your health goals.

Can I change my units?

▼

Yes! Go to Settings and change your unit system (Metric/Imperial), timezone, and date format preferences.

How do I update my profile?

▼

Click on your profile picture/name in the sidebar to open the Profile Settings modal. You can update your personal information, health details, and upload a profile picture.

Is my data secure?

▼

Yes! All your data is encrypted and stored securely. We use JWT authentication and follow best practices for data protection. Your health information is private and confidential.
