Getting Started

Start by chatting with your AI health coach, tracking daily metrics, setting reminders, and reviewing your analytics. Use the quick-action pills in Chat for one-tap logging.

FAQ

How do I track health metrics?

▼

Open Tracking, choose a metric type, enter a value, and tap Add Entry. Quick-action buttons in Chat let you log even faster.

How do reminders work?

▼

Go to Reminders, fill in the title, type, and time. Reminders keep you accountable throughout the day.

Can I change units?

▼

Yes — go to Settings and switch between Metric and Imperial, adjust your timezone, and pick a date format.

How do I update my profile?

▼

Click your name/avatar in the sidebar to open Profile Settings. You can update personal info, health details, and your avatar.

Is my data secure?

▼

All data is encrypted and stored securely using JWT authentication. Your health information is private and never shared.
